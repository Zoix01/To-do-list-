# Art credits

The art collections in Planner's mystery boxes (SPEC §13.13) use only public-domain works. Each image was checked on its Wikimedia Commons page: the painting or print is in the public domain because of its age, and museum scans marked CC0 are dedicated to the public domain by the museum. The images in `public/art/` are resized to WebP (at most 1600 px on the long side); one scan was trimmed to remove its picture frame.
